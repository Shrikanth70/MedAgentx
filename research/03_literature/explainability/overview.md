# Literature Overview: Clinical Explainability & Evidence Traceability

## 1. Domain Summary
Methods to render LLM clinical reasoning transparent, interpretable, and verifiable by clinicians through explicit citation mapping, reasoning step visual traces, and evidence attribution.

## 2. Key Frameworks & Metrics
- **Citation Precision & Recall:** Quantifying whether cited clinical guidelines/papers support the specific claim made.
- **RAG-Checker / Fine-grained Attribution:** Sentence-level claim decomposition and attribution verification.
- **Clinician Cognitive Load:** Ensuring explanations are concise, structured, and clinically actionable rather than verbose text dumps.
