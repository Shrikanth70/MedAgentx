# Literature Overview: Medical & Clinical LLMs

## 1. Domain Summary
Domain-adapted foundation models fine-tuned on clinical literature, electronic health records, guidelines, and medical QA corpora to enhance biomedical reasoning, medical knowledge retrieval, and clinical communication.

## 2. Key Medical LLMs

### Singhal et al. (Google Research, 2025) — *Med-PaLM 2 / Towards Expert-Level Medical Question Answering*
- **Venue:** Nature Medicine 2025 (Preprint 2023) | **DOI:** 10.1038/s41591-024-03423-7
- **Key Results:** Med-PaLM 2 achieved 86.5% accuracy on MedQA (USMLE-style), approaching physician-level accuracy on multiple-choice questions.

### Tu et al. (Google Research, 2025) — *AMIE: Articulate Medical Intelligence Explorer*
- **Venue:** Nature 2025 (Preprint 2024) | **arXiv:** 2401.05654
- **Key Innovation:** Simulated self-play diagnostic dialogue environment for Objective Structured Clinical Examinations (OSCE).

### Labrak et al. (2024) — *BioMistral: A Specialized Open Open-Source Large Language Model for Medical Domains*
- **Venue:** arXiv:2402.10373 (2024)
- **Key Insight:** Open-weights 7B model fine-tuned on PubMed Central, demonstrating strong open-source medical performance.

### OpenBioLLM-70B (Saama AI, 2024)
- Open-source medical LLM fine-tuned on medical instruction datasets, outperforming Med-PaLM 1 on standard benchmarks.
