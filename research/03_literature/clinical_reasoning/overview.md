# Literature Overview: Clinical Reasoning & Differential Diagnosis

## 1. Domain Summary
Formalization of clinical diagnostic reasoning workflows: history taking, symptom aggregation, pathophysiology mapping, differential diagnosis generation, investigation planning, and treatment selection.

## 2. Scientific Principles of Clinical Reasoning
- **System 1 vs. System 2 Reasoning in Medicine:** Fast heuristic recognition vs. analytical multi-step evidence-based reasoning.
- **Differential Diagnosis (DDx) Generation:** Formulating ranked hypotheses prioritized by pre-test probability and life-threat severity.
- **Evidence-Based Medicine (EBM):** Integrating individual clinical expertise with best available external evidence from systematic reviews and clinical practice guidelines.
