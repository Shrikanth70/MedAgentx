# Literature Overview: Clinical AI Safety & Decision Support

## 1. Domain Summary
Focuses on clinical safety, risk governance, regulatory standards (FDA Software as a Medical Device - SaMD), human-in-the-loop oversight, medical error prevention, and trustworthy clinical AI deployment.

## 2. Key Safety & Regulatory Guidelines
- **FDA Guidance on Clinical Decision Support Software (2022):** Requires CDS software to enable health care professionals to independently review the basis for recommendations.
- **Clinician Augmentation vs. Autonomous Diagnosis:** Systems must position themselves as decision support tools that present evidence traces, rather than autonomous diagnostic agents.
- **Hallucination Risks in Medicine:** Incorrect drug dosages, missed contraindications, or false diagnostic assertions can lead to severe adverse clinical events.
