# Literature Overview: Medical RAG Paradigms

## 1. Domain Summary
Domain-specific RAG applications tailored to healthcare datasets, medical literature (PubMed, PMC), clinical practice guidelines (NICE, WHO, CDC), and electronic health record (EHR) text.

## 2. Key Medical RAG Research (2024–2026)

### SEMA-RAG (2026) — *Self-Evolving Multi-Agent RAG for Medical Reasoning*
- Multi-agent architecture for medical QA.

### CLI-RAG (2025) — *Clinical Notes RAG*
- Tailored for clinical EHR summary retrieval and SOAP note generation.

### MedCoAct (2025) — *Confidence-Aware Multi-Agent Collaboration*
- Explores multi-agent collaboration for complete and reliable clinical decision support.
