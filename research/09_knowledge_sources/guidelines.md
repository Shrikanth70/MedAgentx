# 09 Clinical Guidelines & Literature Sources

## Guidelines Repositories
- **NICE Clinical Knowledge Summaries (CKS):** Open access, highly structured primary care management topics.
- **WHO Guidelines Approved by the Guidelines Review Committee:** Open access global public health guidelines.
- **CDC Morbidity and Mortality Weekly Report (MMWR):** Public domain infectious disease and prevention summaries.

## Licensing & Usage
All guidelines selected for MedAgentX indexing are released under Open Access, Public Domain, or CC-BY licenses for non-commercial academic research.
