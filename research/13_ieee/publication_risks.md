# 13 Publication Risks & Mitigation Strategy

## Risk 1: Reviewer perceives 8-agent architecture as arbitrary or over-engineered.
- **Mitigation:** Streamline to 4 core functional modules governed by the ACMR Orchestrator. Explicitly present ablation results proving each module's necessity.

## Risk 2: Reviewer points out competing papers (SEMA-RAG ACL 2026, MedCoAct 2025).
- **Mitigation:** Cite SEMA-RAG and MedCoAct directly in Related Work. Differentiate MedAgentX by highlighting mathematical confidence calibration ($C_{total}$) and Hybrid Graph-Vector traversal.

## Risk 3: Overclaiming clinical autonomy.
- **Mitigation:** Re-position strictly as Clinician Decision Support (CDSS) for clinician augmentation.
