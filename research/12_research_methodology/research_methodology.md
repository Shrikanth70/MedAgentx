# 12 Research Methodology Overview

## 1. Methodological Framework
MedAgentX follows a quantitative, empirical AI benchmarking methodology combined with rigorous comparative ablation analysis and human expert evaluation where feasible.

## 2. Experimental Stages

```
Stage 1: Offline Evaluation on Standardized Medical QA Benchmarks (MedQA, MedMCQA, PubMedQA)
   │
Stage 2: Comparative Baseline Benchmarking against B1–B7
   │
Stage 3: Component-wise Ablation Analysis (Variants A–F)
   │
Stage 4: Failure Mode & Error Analysis
   │
Stage 5: Clinician Usability & Transparency Review
```
