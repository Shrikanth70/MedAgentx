This research repository contains a comprehensive scientific evaluation, literature review, novelty audit, dataset inventory, evaluation framework, architectural design, IEEE readiness assessment, and decision log for **MedAgentX: Adaptive Confidence-Guided Multi-Agent Retrieval-Augmented Framework for Evidence-Based Clinical Decision Support**.
